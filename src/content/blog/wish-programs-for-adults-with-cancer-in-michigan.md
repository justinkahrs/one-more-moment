## Why Families Search for Adult Cancer Wish Programs

Many families know about wish-granting for children, but they may not realize adult wish support can exist. When someone they love is facing Stage IV, advanced, or metastatic cancer, they may start searching quickly because time, health, comfort, travel, and logistics can all feel uncertain.

Those searches often come from a spouse, adult child, family member, friend, caregiver, hospice worker, oncology team member, social worker, church contact, or community member who wants to help but does not know where to start.

Families may use different words for the same hope. They may search for adult wishes with cancer help, granting a wish to adults with cancer, a wish program for adults with Stage 4 cancer, or adult cancer wishes in Michigan. The heart of the search is usually the same: they want to create meaningful time with someone they love.

## What One More Moment Does

One More Moment helps adults in Michigan facing Stage IV, advanced, or metastatic cancer create meaningful Moments with loved ones.

We use the word "Moment" because the experience does not have to look one specific way. A Moment may be a trip, a favorite meal, a sporting event, a family gathering, a quiet experience, time near the water, or something brought to the person when travel is not possible.

The goal is not to make every request large or public. The goal is to help create time, connection, dignity, and memory during a season when those things matter deeply.

Each request is reviewed with care. Submitting a request does not guarantee approval, funding, timing, or availability, but it gives One More Moment the chance to understand the person, the situation, and what kind of Moment may be possible.

## What Kinds of Wishes or Moments May Be Possible?

An adult cancer wish does not have to be elaborate to matter. Many of the most meaningful Moments are centered on time together.

A Moment may include:

- A quiet weekend away
- A favorite meal
- A sporting event
- A family gathering
- A meaningful Michigan destination
- Time near the water
- A special experience with children or grandchildren
- Something brought to the home if travel is not possible

Some Moments involve travel or community partners. Others happen close to home. Some are full of activity and laughter. Others are calm, private, and peaceful.

To see more examples, read [What Types of Wishes Can Adults With Cancer Request?](/blog/what-types-of-wishes-can-adults-with-cancer-request/).

## Who Can Start the Request?

The adult facing cancer can reach out directly, but they do not have to begin the process alone.

A request may be started by:

- A spouse or partner
- An adult child
- A family member
- A friend
- A caregiver
- A hospice worker
- An oncology team member
- A nurse navigator
- A social worker
- A church contact
- A community member

Whenever possible, the person facing cancer should remain central to the request. Their comfort, preferences, consent, privacy, and dignity matter.

## What Families Should Know Before Requesting

You do not need to have everything figured out before asking. A request can begin with what you know, and the details can be reviewed from there.

Helpful details may include:

- The person's diagnosis or general cancer status
- Where they live in Michigan
- What kind of Moment would be meaningful
- Timing concerns
- Travel or accessibility needs
- Who should help coordinate
- Whether the Moment can be shared publicly or should remain private

Timing can matter. Health, comfort, mobility, family availability, weather, and logistics may affect what is possible. If a Moment matters to someone you love, reaching out sooner can give the team more room to understand the request.

## Privacy and Dignity

One More Moment respects the privacy and dignity of every warrior and family we serve.

Some Moments are shared publicly only when permission is given, while many remain private. Receiving a Moment does not require someone to share photos, personal details, or medical information publicly.

Families should share personal and medical information carefully. One More Moment uses submitted information to review eligibility and coordinate requests. You can review the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/) for more information.
