No. An adult cancer wish does not have to involve travel.

A meaningful Moment may happen close to home, somewhere in Michigan, at a favorite restaurant, at a sporting event, near the water, with family, or even at home when travel is difficult.

The value of a Moment is not determined by how far someone travels. Some of the most meaningful experiences can happen close to home.

One More Moment helps adults in Michigan facing Stage IV, advanced, or metastatic cancer create meaningful Moments with the people they love. Families may search for "adult cancer wish," "Make-A-Wish for adults with cancer," "cancer wishes for adults," "adult cancer wish travel," "local cancer wishes," or "Stage 4 cancer wish" because they are trying to understand what kinds of experiences may be possible. One More Moment is an independent Michigan nonprofit and is not affiliated with Make-A-Wish.

For a broader overview, visit our guide to [adult cancer wishes in Michigan](/adult-cancer-wishes-michigan/). If you already know someone who may need support, you can start with the [Request a Moment form](/moments/#request-a-moment).

## Why People Often Think of Trips First

When people hear the word "wish," they may immediately imagine vacations, destination trips, bucket-list travel, or major experiences.

Those experiences can certainly be meaningful. A trip can hold family history, long-held hopes, or the joy of seeing a place someone has always wanted to visit.

But travel is not the only kind of Moment that matters.

For many adults facing advanced cancer, the heart of the wish is not the distance. It is the chance to be with certain people, feel like themselves again, honor a favorite place or tradition, or create a memory that belongs to the family.

## Why Local Moments Can Be Especially Meaningful

A local adult cancer wish can offer practical and emotional benefits, especially when health, timing, and energy are uncertain.

A closer-to-home Moment may mean:

- Less physical strain
- Easier accessibility
- More family participation
- Less travel time
- Greater flexibility
- Easier changes if health shifts
- Familiar surroundings
- More time focused on the experience itself

Advanced cancer can make even simple plans feel complicated. A shorter outing, local gathering, or at-home experience may allow the person to enjoy more of the Moment with less stress.

Local does not mean small. Local means the experience is shaped around the person, their comfort, and the people they love.

## What Can a Local Adult Cancer Wish Look Like?

A local cancer wish may look different for every person and family.

Possible examples may include:

- A favorite restaurant
- A [Detroit sporting event](/blog/can-an-adult-cancer-wish-include-a-sporting-event/)
- A weekend somewhere in Michigan
- A lake or beach day
- A local attraction
- A family gathering
- A favorite outdoor activity
- A special experience near home
- Something brought to the person if travel is not possible

These are examples, not promises. Every request is reviewed based on the person, meaning of the Moment, timing, health considerations, accessibility, location, resources, and what can realistically be coordinated.

If you are exploring ideas, you may also find this guide helpful: [What Types of Wishes Can Adults With Cancer Request?](/blog/what-types-of-wishes-can-adults-with-cancer-request/)

## What About Out-of-State Travel?

One More Moment primarily focuses its resources on creating Moments for adults in Michigan facing Stage IV, advanced, or metastatic cancer.

The organization currently serves adults in Michigan, and requests are reviewed individually. When travel is part of a request, One More Moment specializes in experiences that can realistically be coordinated within Michigan and within the organization's mission, resources, timing, health considerations, and available support.

Staying within Michigan can make it easier to coordinate volunteers, businesses, accessibility needs, transportation, meals, lodging, timing, communication, and backup plans. It can also help One More Moment use donor support responsibly while caring for the person and family at the center of the request.

This does not mean families should be afraid to share the full idea. The first version of a wish can help reveal what matters most.

## What If the Person's Dream Involves Somewhere Far Away?

If the person's dream involves a place far away, families should still explain why the idea matters.

Sometimes the heart of the wish may be recreated or adapted in another meaningful way. For example:

- A destination restaurant may inspire a special meal locally
- A large trip may become a Michigan getaway
- A favorite team experience may happen closer to home
- A place someone loves may inspire a themed family experience

Alternatives cannot always be arranged, and submitting a request does not guarantee approval, funding, timing, travel, or a specific outcome. But understanding the meaning behind the request can help One More Moment explore what may be possible.

For more on why some wishes may need to be adapted, read [Does Every Adult Cancer Wish Get Granted?](/blog/does-every-adult-cancer-wish-get-granted/)

## Health and Travel Considerations

Advanced cancer can affect:

- Energy
- Mobility
- Treatment schedules
- Accessibility
- Transportation
- Weather tolerance
- Time available

A shorter or closer experience can sometimes allow the person to enjoy more of the Moment with less stress.

Families should continue working with oncology, hospice, and other care teams for medical guidance. One More Moment does not provide medical advice, medical transportation, emergency services, or clinical care.

If timing is urgent, read [What to Do When an Adult Cancer Wish Needs to Happen Quickly](/blog/what-to-do-when-an-adult-cancer-wish-needs-to-happen-quickly/)

## The Experience Is About the People, Not the Mileage

One More Moment focuses on creating time, connection, and meaningful memories with loved ones.

The distance traveled does not determine the importance of the Moment.

A dinner close to home can matter. A short drive can matter. A quiet afternoon near the water can matter. A family gathering in the living room can matter. What matters most is whether the experience reflects the person, brings loved ones together, and gives the family a memory they can carry.

Some Moments are joyful and active. Some are calm and private. Some happen in public places. Many remain known only to the family. A Moment does not need to look impressive from the outside to be meaningful on the inside.

## Families Should Still Share the Original Idea

Families do not need to edit themselves before reaching out.

When you contact One More Moment, share:

- What the person hopes to do
- Why it matters
- Who they want with them
- Any accessibility concerns
- Timing
- How flexible the idea may be

The original idea helps the team understand the person behind the request. Even if the exact request is not possible, the meaning behind it may point toward another Moment that still feels personal and dignified.

## Privacy and Dignity

Some Moments are shared publicly only when permission is given, while many remain private. One More Moment respects the privacy and dignity of every warrior and family we serve.

Receiving a Moment does not require someone to share photos, personal details, or medical information publicly. Families should share personal and medical information carefully. One More Moment uses submitted information to review eligibility and coordinate requests. You can review the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/) for more information.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do adult cancer wishes have to involve travel?

No. An adult cancer wish does not have to involve travel. A meaningful Moment may happen close to home, somewhere in Michigan, at a favorite restaurant, near the water, at a sporting event, with family, or at home when travel is difficult.

### Can an adult cancer wish be local?

Yes. Local cancer wishes can be deeply meaningful. A shorter or closer experience may reduce physical strain, make family participation easier, allow more flexibility, and help the person focus on the Moment itself.

### Does One More Moment provide adult cancer wishes in Michigan?

One More Moment helps adults in Michigan facing Stage IV, advanced, or metastatic cancer create meaningful Moments with loved ones. Every request is reviewed individually.

### Is One More Moment affiliated with Make-A-Wish?

No. One More Moment is not affiliated with Make-A-Wish. One More Moment is an independent Michigan nonprofit with its own mission, eligibility guidelines, review process, and focus on adults in Michigan facing Stage IV, advanced, or metastatic cancer.

### Can families request out-of-state travel?

Families may share what would be meaningful, including an idea connected to a faraway place. One More Moment primarily focuses its resources on Moments that can realistically be coordinated within Michigan. If a faraway request is not realistic, the team may explore a local or closer-to-home option when possible.

### What if the wish needs to happen quickly?

If time is limited, families should reach out as soon as possible and explain the timing, health considerations, accessibility needs, and what would matter most. Some urgent requests may need to be simplified or adapted.
