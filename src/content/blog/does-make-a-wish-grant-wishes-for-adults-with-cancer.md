Many families search for "Make-A-Wish for adults with cancer" because Make-A-Wish is the wish-granting name they know. They may be trying to understand whether support exists for an adult they love who is facing advanced cancer, and they may not know what to search for next.

Make-A-Wish is widely known for helping children with critical illnesses. One More Moment is not affiliated with Make-A-Wish. One More Moment is an independent Michigan nonprofit focused on helping adults facing Stage IV, advanced, or metastatic cancer create meaningful Moments with loved ones.

If you are looking for an adult cancer wish, an adult Make-A-Wish alternative, or a wish program for adults with Stage 4 cancer in Michigan, One More Moment may be a helpful place to begin.

For a broader overview, visit our guide to [adult cancer wishes in Michigan](/adult-cancer-wishes-michigan/). If you already know someone who may need support, you can start with the [Request a Moment form](/moments/#request-a-moment).

## Why Families Search This Question

When an adult is facing advanced cancer, families may not know where to turn. They may want to create one meaningful day, one quiet weekend, one visit, one gathering, or one experience that gives everyone time together while it matters deeply.

In that moment, many people search for the organization name they recognize. They may type "does Make-A-Wish grant wishes for adults with cancer," "Make-A-Wish for adults with cancer," "adult Make-A-Wish," or "make a wish for adults with cancer" because they are trying to describe the kind of help they hope exists.

That search usually comes from love and urgency. It may come from a spouse, adult child, caregiver, friend, hospice worker, oncology team member, social worker, church contact, or community member who wants to help but is not sure what is possible.

The important answer is this: adult wish-granting support does exist, but it is usually provided by independent organizations with their own missions, locations, guidelines, and review processes.

## Is There a Wish Program for Adults With Cancer?

Yes. Independent adult wish-granting nonprofits do exist.

One More Moment helps adults in Michigan facing Stage IV, advanced, or metastatic cancer create meaningful Moments with the people they love. A Moment may be simple, quiet, joyful, practical, or personal. The goal is not to copy another organization. The goal is to help protect time, connection, dignity, and memory for adults and families navigating late-stage cancer.

Every request is reviewed with care. Submitting a request does not guarantee approval, funding, timing, or availability, but it gives One More Moment the chance to understand the person, the situation, and what kind of Moment may be meaningful.

If you are not sure whether someone qualifies, read [What If I'm Not Sure Someone Qualifies for an Adult Cancer Wish?](/blog/what-if-im-not-sure-someone-qualifies-for-an-adult-cancer-wish/).

## What Kind of Wishes Can Adults Request?

An adult cancer wish does not have to be large or public to matter. For many families, the most meaningful Moment is centered on time together.

A Moment may include:

- A quiet family weekend away
- A favorite meal
- A sporting event
- Time near the water
- A family gathering
- A meaningful local experience
- Something brought to the home if travel is not possible

Some Moments involve travel or local partners. Others happen close to home. Some are full of laughter and activity. Others are calm, private, and deeply personal.

The size of the Moment does not decide its meaning. What matters is whether the Moment reflects the person facing cancer and gives them meaningful time with the people they love.

## Who Can Start the Request?

A request may begin with the adult facing cancer, but they do not have to navigate the process alone.

People who may help start a request include:

- A spouse or partner
- An adult child
- A family member
- A friend
- A caregiver
- A hospice worker
- An oncology team member
- A nurse navigator
- A social worker
- A church contact
- A community member

Whenever possible, the person facing cancer should remain central to the request. Their comfort, preferences, dignity, privacy, and consent matter.

## Who Qualifies for a One More Moment Wish?

One More Moment generally serves adults in Michigan facing Stage IV, advanced, or metastatic cancer.

The team reviews each request individually and may consider the person's diagnosis, Michigan connection, requested Moment, timing, logistics, safety, available resources, and whether additional information is needed.

If you are trying to understand adult cancer wish eligibility, visit [Who Qualifies for a One More Moment Wish?](/blog/who-qualifies-for-a-one-more-moment-wish/).

## Privacy and Dignity Matter

One More Moment respects the privacy and dignity of every warrior and family we serve.

Some Moments are shared publicly only when permission is given, while many remain private. Receiving a Moment does not require someone to share photos, personal details, or medical information publicly.

Families should share personal and medical information carefully. One More Moment uses submitted information to review eligibility and coordinate requests. You can review the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/) for more information.
