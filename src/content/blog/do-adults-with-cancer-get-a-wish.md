Yes, some adults with cancer may be able to receive a wish or meaningful Moment through independent adult wish-granting organizations like One More Moment.

One More Moment serves adults in Michigan facing Stage IV, advanced, or metastatic cancer. The organization helps create meaningful Moments with loved ones, centered on time, connection, dignity, and care.

One More Moment is not affiliated with Make-A-Wish. Make-A-Wish is widely known for helping children with critical illnesses, which is why many families search phrases like "do adults with cancer get a wish" or "Make-A-Wish for adults with cancer" when they are trying to find support for an adult they love.

For a broader overview, visit our guide to [adult cancer wishes in Michigan](/adult-cancer-wishes-michigan/). If you already know someone who may need support, you can start with the [Request a Moment form](/moments/#request-a-moment).

## Why Families Ask This Question

Many people know about wish-granting for children, but they may not realize support may also exist for adults. When someone is facing advanced cancer, families often begin searching quickly because they want to help a spouse, parent, sibling, adult child, friend, patient, or neighbor experience something meaningful.

That search may come from love, urgency, uncertainty, or all three. Families may not know what kind of wish is possible, whether an adult can qualify, whether travel is realistic, or who is allowed to start the conversation.

The important answer is that adult cancer wish support does exist through independent organizations with their own missions, service areas, eligibility guidelines, and review processes.

## What Is an Adult Cancer Wish?

At One More Moment, we often use the word "Moment" because the experience does not have to look one specific way. A Moment may be large or simple, public or private, close to home or away for a short time.

An adult cancer wish may be a trip, a favorite meal, a sporting event, a family gathering, a quiet experience, time near the water, or something brought to someone when travel is not possible.

What matters most is that the Moment reflects the person facing cancer and creates meaningful time with the people they love.

To learn more about what these experiences can look like, read [What Types of Wishes Can Adults With Cancer Request?](/blog/what-types-of-wishes-can-adults-with-cancer-request/).

## Who May Qualify?

One More Moment generally helps adults in Michigan facing Stage IV, advanced, or metastatic cancer.

Each request is reviewed with care. The team may consider the person's diagnosis, Michigan connection, requested Moment, timing, logistics, safety, available resources, and whether additional information is needed.

If you are trying to understand adult cancer wish eligibility, visit [Who Qualifies for a One More Moment Wish?](/blog/who-qualifies-for-a-one-more-moment-wish/).

## Who Can Request or Start the Conversation?

The adult facing cancer can reach out directly, but they do not have to carry the process alone.

A request may begin with:

- A spouse or partner
- An adult child
- A family member
- A friend
- A caregiver
- A hospice worker
- An oncology team member
- A nurse navigator
- A social worker
- A church contact
- A community member

Whenever possible, the person facing cancer should remain central to the request. Their comfort, preferences, consent, privacy, and dignity matter.

If you are not sure whether someone qualifies or whether it is okay to ask, read [What If I'm Not Sure Someone Qualifies for an Adult Cancer Wish?](/blog/what-if-im-not-sure-someone-qualifies-for-an-adult-cancer-wish/).

## What Kind of Wishes Can Adults With Cancer Request?

An adult cancer wish does not have to be elaborate to matter. Many of the most meaningful Moments are centered on protected time together.

A Moment may include:

- A quiet weekend away
- A favorite meal
- A sporting event
- Time near the water
- A family gathering
- A meaningful local experience
- Something brought to the home if travel is not possible

Some Moments involve travel or community partners. Others happen close to home. Some are active and joyful. Others are quiet, private, and peaceful.

Submitting a request does not guarantee approval, funding, timing, or availability. It does give One More Moment the chance to understand the person, the situation, and what kind of Moment may be possible.

## Why Timing Matters

When someone is facing advanced cancer, timing can affect what is possible. Health, comfort, accessibility, travel ability, treatment schedules, family availability, weather, and logistics can all shape a request.

Families do not need to wait until they have the perfect plan. In many cases, it is better to reach out sooner with what you know. The request can begin as a simple conversation, and the details can be reviewed from there.

If a Moment matters to someone you love, starting early gives the team more room to understand the request and consider what may be realistic.

## Privacy and Dignity

One More Moment respects the privacy and dignity of every warrior and family we serve.

Some Moments are shared publicly only when permission is given, while many remain private. Receiving a Moment does not require someone to share photos, personal details, or medical information publicly.

Families should share personal and medical information carefully. One More Moment uses submitted information to review eligibility and coordinate requests. You can review the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/) for more information.

## How to Request a Moment

If you know an adult in Michigan facing Stage IV, advanced, or metastatic cancer who may benefit from a meaningful Moment, you can start by submitting a request or contacting One More Moment.

You do not need the perfect words. You do not need to know whether the request is big enough. You do not need every date, cost, or detail finalized before beginning.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do adults with cancer get a wish?

Some adults with cancer may be able to receive a wish or meaningful Moment through independent adult wish-granting organizations. One More Moment helps adults in Michigan facing Stage IV, advanced, or metastatic cancer create meaningful Moments with loved ones.

### Is One More Moment affiliated with Make-A-Wish?

No. One More Moment is not affiliated with Make-A-Wish. One More Moment is an independent Michigan nonprofit with its own mission, eligibility guidelines, review process, and focus on adults facing Stage IV, advanced, or metastatic cancer.

### What is an adult cancer wish?

An adult cancer wish is a meaningful experience centered on time, connection, and care. It may be a quiet weekend away, favorite meal, sporting event, time near the water, family gathering, meaningful local experience, or something brought to the home if travel is not possible.

### Who can start a request?

The adult facing cancer may reach out directly, and a spouse, adult child, family member, friend, caregiver, hospice worker, oncology team member, social worker, church contact, or community member may help begin the request.

### What if I am not sure someone qualifies?

It is still okay to ask. One More Moment reviews each request with care. You can read [What If I'm Not Sure Someone Qualifies for an Adult Cancer Wish?](/blog/what-if-im-not-sure-someone-qualifies-for-an-adult-cancer-wish/) or begin with the [Request a Moment form](/moments/#request-a-moment).
